Axiomancer: Table Edition — assets

Rendered card art for virtual tabletops. 63 sheets · 664 cards · generated 2026-08-13.

What this is. Every virtual tabletop imports a deck the same way: one grid image plus its column and row count, sliced in reading order. These are those images, with the numbers each one needs.

Filenames carry a content hash (deck-malison.a1b2c3d4.png) because Tabletop Simulator caches by URL permanently. A changed sheet gets a new URL and refetches; an unchanged one keeps its URL and stays cached.

Machine-readable index: manifest.json — every sheet with its grid, cell size, back group and hash.

Reading the grids

fieldmeaning
gridcolumns × rows. Importers slice the whole rectangle in reading order.
trailing blanksSheets are padded to a full rectangle. Those cells are blank on purpose. Tabletop Simulator lets a deck list an arbitrary subset of cells, so they simply never become cards; importers that take the whole grid will need them deleted.
backWhich back sheet the deck uses. This is a rule, not a preference: a card's back follows the deck it is shuffled into, or it is a marked card in that deck.
